### ما هي المنصة؟

**KidsNotices** منصة سحابية (SaaS) متعددة المستأجرين (Multi-Tenant) — كل مؤسسة تعليمية تحصل على فضائها المستقل والآمن الخاص بها على نفس المنصة — مصمَّمة خصيصاً للمدارس والمجموعات المدرسية الخصوصية بالمغرب. تربط المنصة **الإدارة، الأساتذة، أولياء الأمور، التلاميذ، الحراسة العامة، والسائقين** في مكان واحد، بواجهة كاملة بالعربية والفرنسية (مع دعم الكتابة من اليمين لليسار)، متاحة عبر المتصفح، كتطبيق ويب مثبَّت (PWA)، وكتطبيق أندرويد مخصَّص.

## 🇬🇧 In English

### What is KidsNotices?

**KidsNotices** is a multi-tenant cloud (SaaS) platform built for private schools and school groups in Morocco: each school gets its own secure, independent space. It brings together **the administration, teachers, parents, students, supervisors and bus drivers** in one place, with a fully bilingual Arabic/French interface (right-to-left supported), available in the browser, as an installable web app (PWA) and as an Android app.

Highlights: weekly timetables, attendance, grades compatible with the Massar import/export format, lessons and homework with instant parent notifications, two-way messaging, live school-bus tracking, canteen menus and orders, monthly fees and teacher pay, health records and QR-protected pick-ups, the annual school census, and daily per-school backups.

### 🤖 New: KidsNotices AI — the AI assistant for your school

An AI assistant that understands **Arabic, Moroccan Darija and French**, built into the parent, teacher and admin spaces:

- 💬 **Parents get instant answers**, day and night, about absences, grades, homework, payments and transport — only from their own children's data.
- 🔎 **Google-style smart search** across the platform: a direct answer, then the right link, with voice search.
- ✍️ **Arabic and French announcements in seconds** from two lines of notes, plus translation using Moroccan school vocabulary.
- 📝 **Teacher assistant**: report-card comments and exercise ideas matched to the lesson and level.
- 📊 **Smart report for the principal** from absence and unpaid-fee figures, with alerts and recommendations.
- 🔐 **Student privacy first**: names are replaced with codes before reaching the AI, phone numbers and Massar codes are removed, and conversation text is not stored.
- 💰 **Full cost control**: limits per school, per feature and per user, with a clear usage dashboard.
